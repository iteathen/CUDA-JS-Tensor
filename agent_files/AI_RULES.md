# AI Rules

- Diagnose before editing and verify after every coherent mutation.
- Prefer the smallest complete public contract over a universal manager object.
- Make hidden shape, dtype, layout, ownership, synchronization, and lifecycle assumptions explicit.
- Keep mathematical semantics separate from backend selection and launch realization.
- Do not infer implementation authority from a roadmap, research note, open issue, or passing experiment.
- Do not expose CUDA-JS native capabilities, generated CUDA source, machine paths, device identifiers, or provider objects.
- Do not represent mocks, source inspection, or Windows evidence as native Linux or multi-GPU qualification.
- Do not touch `the_restaurant` while its integration lane is deferred.
- Preserve exact errors and cleanup truth without leaking private provider details.
- Report limits honestly and keep the worktree, repository, remote branches, issues, PRs, and generated artifacts clean.
